
高级长难句5：
Yet the researcn revealea that the U.S. factories of Honda, Nissan, and Toyota achieved about 95 percent of the productivity of their Japanese counterpart — a result of the training that U.S. workers received on the job.

三脱法：脱掉介词短语“of Honda, Nissan, and Toyota”，脱掉介词短语“of the productivity of their Japanese counterpart”，脱掉插入语“a result of the training that U.S. workers received on the job”。句子主干为“Yet the researcn revealea that the U.S. factories achieved about 95 percent”（研究显示美国工厂实现了95的百分比）

逻辑点：
（1）the U.S. factories of Honda, Nissan, and Toyota翻译为“Honda, Nissan, and Toyota”这三者的美国工厂，其中“，”“and”都是并列这三个词。
（2）the productivity of their Japanese counterpart：their指代n，就近往前找n，their为复数，则就近往前找复数n，指代“Honda, Nissan, and Toyota”，“counterpart”是对立物，由于Japanese 与 U.S. 相对应，故counterpart应该指代“factories”，即“Honda, Nissan, and Toyota的日本工厂”。
（3）A is a result of B：A是结果，B是原因，可以翻译为 A因为B。

三脱法找句子主干：脱插入语、脱介词、脱非谓语、脱从句（要注意，脱从句的时候是要先处理后面的从句的，即是从后往前进行处理从句的），本句子中有两个从句，分别由that引导，where引导。先处理后面的where从句，脱去“where newspapers are least distinctive”，然后处理前面的that从句，脱去“that swept through newsrooms”。
